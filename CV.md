## Interview talking points

1. **Why integers and bigint?** A float can't represent 0.1 exactly, but the real bug is rounding each share separately: £100 ÷ 3 becomes £99.99. Largest-remainder allocation gives the missing penny to someone.
2. **Banker's rounding:** half-up adds a small upward bias to every tie. Half-even removes it on average (mean round-trip error −0.00012 units vs +0.00016 for floats, both tiny).
3. **Honest negatives:** floats tied on round trips and running balances, and exact settle-up rarely beats greedy. I reported both rather than hiding them.
4. **Avoiding overfitting:** the categoriser and detector were tuned on dev seeds and reported on separate test seeds.
5. **Virtualisation and accessibility:** only ~18 rows exist in the DOM, so screen readers get `aria-rowcount` and `aria-rowindex` to know the real size, and the scroll region is focusable for keyboard users.
